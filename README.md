I am a **Machine Learning Engineer** and **Blockchain Developer** passionate about pushing the boundaries of what's possible with hardware-constrained AI and decentralized networks. 

- 🔭 I’m currently building **WorldNgen** (AI-generated 3D worlds on-chain) and **Glimpse** (Multimodal Arweave Search).
- 🏆 **Hackathon Winner:** Smart India Hackathon (Govt of India), Agents of the Permaweb, Neo X Grind.
- 🔬 **Research Focus:** Unsupervised Learning, Multimodal Embeddings (ImageBind/CLIP), and Decentralized Compute (AO/Solana).
